## Locked V1 scope
Auth + profile | monthly target | customers | daily plan | pre-visit | visit and sales recording | follow-ups | route sequence + Google Maps links | REPLAN | TARGET RESCUE | health check-in | end-of-day review | Coach | pilot feedback.

## Non-goals
No manager dashboard, attendance, background GPS, payroll/HRMS, full CRM, paid maps/AI API, or app-store release in the 30-day pilot.

## Principles
Deterministic target/planning math. AI optional and replaceable. Home-time protected. Never guarantee targets. Never fabricate data.
